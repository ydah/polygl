# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, appearance, or ability.
Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks and intimidation are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and patient.
- Critique code, contracts, and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing security reports or personal information.
- Accept a maintainer decision after the rationale and re-entry conditions are
  recorded, while remaining free to propose new evidence later.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, unwanted
sexual attention, and retaliation are unacceptable in project spaces or when
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and
fairly. Maintainers may correct, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban
participation according to severity and pattern. A maintainer involved in a
report must recuse themself where another maintainer is available.

This policy applies to repositories, reviews, issue trackers, community spaces,
and public representation of the project.
